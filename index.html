Welcome to
Sparkle and Shine!

Sparkle and Shine is a design service in NYC. Whether it’s an invitation or a flyer for a group, we always try to make your order special!

What we make

Every order is drawn up from scratch for you — tell us the colours, the names and the date, and we’ll take it from there.

Invitations

Birthdays, quinceañeras, showers, christenings — printed or sent straight to phones.

Flyers

For a group, a team, a fundraiser or a shop — something people actually want to pick up.

Something else?

Got an idea that isn’t on this list? Send it over and we’ll tell you if we can make it.

How we
do things

Here we have three easy steps to order and get your design.

  1. #1

    Step 1. Email us about your request and your criteria. We’ll work as hard as we can to make your design.

  2. #2

    Step 2. Your design takes a few days at most, so it comes out its best.

  3. #3

    Step 3. We’ll then email the finished result to you and you can tell us if you have any other orders you may want to make.

Who you’ll see

One person doing the designs right here in the Bronx, and we’ll help you make your designs special.

Collab!

In collaboration with Cientificos Agency. We’ll design your site, and the Cientificos make it go live and promote it to the world!

Questions?

Tell us what you need and we’ll get back to you. The more you can say about colours, names and dates, the better the first draft will be.

Thank you!

Your message is on its way. We’ll reply to your email as soon as we can — usually within a day.
